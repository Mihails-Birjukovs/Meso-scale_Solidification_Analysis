# Research, authorship, provenance and rights

## Primary reference

M. Birjukovs, N. Shevchenko and S. Eckert, "An image processing pipeline for in situ dynamic x-ray imaging of directional solidification of metal alloys in thin cells," *Experiments in Fluids* **64**, 131 (2023). [DOI 10.1007/s00348-023-03671-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00348-023-03671-2).

The article documents the historical Mathematica scientific pipeline. It must not be cited as a validation of later Python source-relative intensity thresholds, user-reviewed masks, the proposed component graph, or optical-flow energy budget closure.

## Experimental data and institutional identity

Original source radiographs, full provenance manifests, camera/thermal records, private working directories, submitted source review PNGs and independent phase annotations are not bundled with this public documentation. Publication of code **does not** confer rights to redistribute experimental images or institutional artwork.

This website adopts the **INM-inspired color/layout template** using CSS and **text-only identity**, with **no official INM logo or official institutional symbol** until separately approved. It does not imply an institutional endorsement or transfer of any ISSP/OOC or HZDR image rights.

## Research boundaries

- **Documented:** published paper, the main-branch software inventory, and explicitly linked draft methods.
- **Conditionally accepted:** experiment-owner visual judgments for selected provisional morphology frames.
- **Planned:** T1 temporal component graph and optional T2 structured statistical labels.
- **Not accepted:** a fitted physical phase fraction, absolute indium concentration, full MIL equivalence or real-flow energy closure.

For reproducible research inquiries, identify the source code commit and data rights/experiment you need; see the corresponding article and project issue tracker.
